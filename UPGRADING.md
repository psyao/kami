# Upgrading

Every release has a section here, newest first (before 0.9.1, breaking releases only): what changed, why, and what a site does about it, or that it has nothing to do. `## Unreleased` gathers what the coming release holds so far. A site updates with `composer update psyao/kami`, then deploys as usual ([Deployment](docs/guide/deployment.md)).

- **Versions.** Before 1.0, a breaking release bumps the minor (`0.1.x` to `0.2.0`) and everything else is a patch, so `^0.1` receives every non-breaking change. From 1.0, a breaking release is a major.
- **Public contract.** The public surface (`docs/reference/`), the content events and their properties, the keys of `config/kami.php`, the props of `x-kami::` components, route names and `kami.*` permissions. Anything marked `@internal` or under an `Internal` namespace may change in any release.
- **Deprecations.** A deprecated element carries `@deprecated since x.y, use …` and a line here and in `CHANGELOG.md`. Nothing warns at runtime: Larastan and IDEs flag it. It is removed in the next major, after at least one minor in which it was deprecated.
- **Upgrade floor.** From 1.0, a release that squashes Kami's migrations is a major, and its section names the release a site must reach first.
- **Security fixes.** A patch that tightens a default for security reasons is listed here too, though it breaks the rule above.

## Unreleased

1. **Nested Entries' Paths hold their ancestors' slugs.** A child Page answers at `/parent/child`, no longer at `/child`, and an Entry under a Draft or Scheduled ancestor answers 404, its `url()` returning `null`. The next `kami:install` rewrites the Paths of nested Entries; a former address once public answers 301 to the new one. Until then, a nested Entry answers 404: run `kami:install` in the same deploy, as usual. A site with no nested Entries has nothing to do ([Paths](docs/guide/routing-and-rendering.md#paths)).
2. **A Term's page lists the Entries linking it.** A Taxonomy's `render()` now receives `$entries`, its Term Archive, and its default view gets `$term` and `$entries` beside `$entry` ([Term Archives](docs/guide/routing-and-rendering.md#term-archives)). A Taxonomy of yours overriding `render(Entry $entry)` no longer loads: PHP requires the override to take the new optional parameter, so declare `render(Entry $entry, ?CursorPaginator $entries = null)`, with `use Illuminate\Contracts\Pagination\CursorPaginator;`. A type's Archive, the list of its Entries at its Prefix, is opt-in with `protected bool $archive = true` ([Archives](docs/guide/content-types.md#archives)): a site that declares none has nothing else to do.

## 0.9.1

0.9.1 changes nothing a site's Form does today, unless it declares a method named `confirmation()`.

1. **A Form can be submitted without a reload.** The Form route answers JSON to a request that expects it, for a script of your own, which the Forms guide gives to copy: the Confirmation in place, the errors beside their fields, no reload and the page still cached ([Without a reload](docs/guide/forms.md#without-a-reload)). Without the script, nothing changes. The Confirmation comes from `Form::confirmation()`, `kami-forms.{handle}.confirmation` or Kami's own: a view that printed its own string on `?sent=` can print `Kami::form('contact')->confirmation()` instead, so both paths read one text. A Form made by `kami:make:form` before 0.9.1 keeps that string under `kami-forms.{handle}.sent`: rename the key `confirmation` in each `lang/<locale>/kami-forms.php`, or `confirmation()` reads Kami's own text instead of yours. `confirmation()` is `final` on `Form`: a Form of yours declaring a method of that name no longer loads, so rename that method; it is the one change that can stop a site, and no Form Kami generated has one.

## 0.9.0

0.9.0 breaks nothing a site declared, but it is the release a site switches over with, and some of its fixes change what a site sees or must have.

1. **Kami's migrations are append-only from 0.9.0** ([ADR 0035](docs/adr/0035-package-migrations-append-only-squashed-only-in-a-major.md)). A released migration never changes again; a schema change comes as a new one, which `kami:install` runs as usual. 0.9.0 adds `kami_import_keys`, for [Imports](docs/guide/imports.md).
2. **A panel without `->passwordReset()` cannot give a set-password link.** `SetPasswordLink::make()` now throws a `LogicException` saying so, where it used to fail on a missing route; `kami:install` already refused. Add `->passwordReset()` to the panel's provider, as the starter kit has ([Users and Roles](docs/guide/users-and-roles.md)). The link now opens a page that reads "Set your password", unless the panel serves a reset page of its own. A Users screen copied from the starter kit before 0.9 shows the link as plain text in its notification: copy the starter kit's `CreateUser` page, whose notification has a button that copies the link (its `copy` action), with the `link.copy` and `link.copied` lines of `lang/<locale>/users.php`.
3. **Labels read as translated.** Kami's Resources and a site's Content Types no longer title-case their labels: "Journal d’audit", not "Journal D’audit". A Resource of yours copied from the starter kit before 0.9, its Users screen for one, keeps title-casing until it sets `protected static bool $hasTitleCaseModelLabel = false;`, as the starter kit now does. Filament puts the singular label mid-sentence, so a capital written there shows: "Créer Page". Write each type's `label` in `lang/<locale>/kami-types.php` in lower case where the language does (`'label' => 'page'`); its `plural`, and a Singleton's or Global Set's `label`, keep their capital ([Labels](docs/guide/content-types.md#labels)). A Users screen copied from the starter kit does the same in `lang/<locale>/users.php` (`'label' => 'utilisateur'`). A missing label now falls back to the handle in sentence case: `news_item` reads `News item`, no longer `News Item`.
4. **A Form on a page in another Locale than English needs Laravel's validation messages in it.** Without them, a French page shows `The nom field is required.`. Install them once with `composer require laravel-lang/common --dev` and `php artisan lang:add fr` ([Declaring a Form](docs/guide/forms.md#declaring-a-form)). `old()` now holds a field's value in the shape its rules allow: a view reading a field that was posted as `name[]=x` gets nothing back rather than an array, and a checkbox group whose only rule is `tags.*`, posted as `tags=b`, gets nothing back rather than a string.
5. **A failed delivery shows at once.** `kami:doctor` warns about a Submission delivery that failed as soon as it failed, not a minute later, so a deploy running `--strict` right after a failure stops there; `kami:forms:deliver` retries it at its next run and says when that is, in UTC (`next try at 17:45 UTC`), as `kami:doctor` prints its times ([Retries](docs/guide/forms.md#retries)).
6. **`ContentMigrator` refuses an unknown Field.** `renameField()`, `makeTranslatable()` and `makeShared()` throw on a name that is neither a column, a declared relation Field nor a handle the type's Entries hold links under, instead of recording the migration as done with nothing moved ([Schema changes](docs/guide/content-types.md#schema-changes)). A migration already run is not run again; one not yet run on an environment, with a mistyped name, now fails there: correct the name.

## 0.8.0

1. **Kami's Scheduled tasks are declared by `Kami::schedule()`.** Kami no longer registers `kami:sitemap` on Laravel's scheduler by itself, and `kami:install` no longer rebuilds a stale sitemap on each deploy. Add the line to `routes/console.php`, as the starter kit now does, or the sitemap stays stale once built:

   ```php
   use Psyao\Kami\Facades\Kami;

   Kami::schedule();
   ```

   It declares every Kami task, each listed with its frequency in [Scheduler](docs/guide/scheduler.md): three new ones, `kami:announce-publications` every minute, which dispatches `EntryPublished` for a Scheduled Translation once its moment has come, `kami:forms:deliver` every 15 minutes, which retries the delivery of Submissions, and `kami:forms:prune` daily, their retention; then the hourly sitemap rebuild and the pruning of Preview links, old page cache files, the Audit log and stale Conversions. A site that keeps its own scheduler instead declares these three too, or Scheduled Translations never announce their publication and a failed delivery is never retried. On a server, replace `schedule:run` in the crontab with `php artisan kami:tick`, which runs every overdue task, Kami's and your own, in-process. A cron entry of yours running `kami:audit:prune`, `kami:media:prune` or `model:prune` for Preview links can go.
2. **The `smtp` mailer needs a timeout.** `kami:doctor` warns, so `--strict` fails, whenever the default mailer is an `smtp` one with no `timeout`, outside `APP_ENV=local` and `testing`: a hung mail server would hold a PHP worker for 60 s on each Submission or password reset email. Laravel's own `config/mail.php` sets `'timeout' => null`, so a site made before 0.8 fails, a deploy script running `--strict` included. Set the line on the `smtp` mailer in `config/mail.php`, as the starter kit now does, and `MAIL_TIMEOUT=10` in `.env` ([Mail](docs/guide/hosting.md#mail)):

   ```php
   'timeout' => env('MAIL_TIMEOUT') ?: null,
   ```

   Without `?: null`, a blank `MAIL_TIMEOUT=` throws at send time.
3. **`kami.forms` is a new config key.** A `config/kami.php` published before 0.8 lacks it: add `'forms' => [],` to list your Forms there ([Forms](docs/guide/forms.md#declaring-a-form)).

## 0.7.0

1. **Pages are cached.** With `APP_DEBUG` off, a page Kami renders is stored under `storage/framework/kami/pages/` and served again until a core write, a `kami:install`, the next scheduled publication or one hour at most ([Page cache](docs/guide/page-cache.md)). A page showing the time of day or something per visitor calls `Kami::withoutPageCache()` while it renders; code writing data a page shows without a core action, your application's tables for instance, calls `Kami::flushCache()` after it. To keep every page rendered per request, set `kami.page_cache.enabled` to `false`. `kami:doctor` warns, so `--strict` fails, when Kami cannot write to that directory, page cache on or off: `Kami::remember()` then caches nothing. `optimize:clear` now also clears Kami's cache.
2. **`summary()` describes pages.** `ContentType` gains a public `summary(Entry $entry): ?string`, and `Entry` a `summary()` that calls it ([Description](docs/guide/seo.md#description)). A type of yours that already declares a `summary()` method with another signature now fails to load: rename it, or give it that signature. A Field named `summary` keeps reading as `$entry->summary`. `kami:doctor` warns, so `--strict` fails, about a type with a URL, the default `summary()` and no RichText Field: override `summary()` in it, returning `null` if it has nothing to say.
3. **The Site settings gain `default_description`.** `kami:install` adds its column. An `EntryForm` of yours for `SiteSettings` places the new Field, or hides it with `->hidden()`, or the Site settings screen throws ([Your own form](docs/guide/content-types.md#your-own-form)).
4. **`seo_image` is reserved, and the Site settings gain `default_social_image`.** An Entry's Social image is written through `SaveEntry` under `seo_image` ([Social image](docs/guide/seo.md#social-image)). A Content Type declaring a Field with the handle `seo_image` now fails the first time the types are read: rename the Field, and move its stored values with `ContentMigrator::renameField()` in a migration. An `EntryForm` of yours for `SiteSettings` places `default_social_image`, or hides it with `->hidden()`, as in item 3. `ContentType` gains a public `socialImage(Entry $entry): ?Media`: a type of yours that already declares a `socialImage()` method with another signature now fails to load. The `kami-social` Conversion is now 1200 × 630, cut at 40:21 instead of 1.91:1, so its URLs change once.
5. **Forms of a type with a URL end with an SEO section.** The generated form of every type with a URL, Terms included, now ends with a collapsed SEO section holding `meta_title`, `meta_description`, `noindex` and `seo_image` ([The SEO section](docs/guide/seo.md#the-seo-section)). An `EntryForm` of yours that builds its own list, without `parent::components()`, places it with `$this->seo()`, or the edit screen throws ([Your own form](docs/guide/content-types.md#your-own-form)). A form extending `parent::components()` has it already.
6. **Old addresses redirect, and Kami's own segments are reserved.** A slug or Prefix change on a Translation public once, even rescheduled to a later date since, now keeps its former address as a 301 to the Entry, instead of answering 404 (`kami:install` adds the `was_public` column that remembers it); that address stays taken until another Entry's Path replaces it ([Changing an address](docs/guide/routing-and-rendering.md#changing-an-address)). `sitemaps` and `kami` join `media` as reserved first segments, and `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` as reserved whole Paths: a Content Type whose Prefix takes one now fails the first time the types are read, so set another `$prefix`; a slug taking one is refused at its next save, in every Locale. `kami:doctor` warns, so `--strict` fails, about a stored Path of the default Locale hidden by a Locale code or a reserved segment: change its slug ([Reserved paths](docs/guide/routing-and-rendering.md#reserved-paths)).
7. **Bring your site's front up to M7.** Nothing breaks without these steps, but a site made from the starter kit before 0.7 gets M7's front only through them, as the starter kit now ships them:
   - in your layout, `<x-kami::seo />` in place of the `<title>` tag, for the title, description, canonical URL, robots, language links and social card ([The head](docs/guide/seo.md#the-head));
   - in `public/index.php`, right after Laravel's maintenance check, the line that serves a cached page before Laravel boots ([Serving before Laravel boots](docs/guide/page-cache.md#serving-before-laravel-boots));
   - in `bootstrap/app.php`, `Kami::exceptions($exceptions);` first in `withExceptions()`, for error pages in the visitor's Locale ([Error pages](docs/guide/routing-and-rendering.md#error-pages));
   - `/public/sitemap.xml` and `/public/sitemaps` in `.gitignore`: `kami:install` now builds them ([Sitemap](docs/guide/seo.md#sitemap));
   - delete Laravel's default `public/robots.txt`, which lacks Kami's first line, then run `kami:install`: it writes Kami's in its place, so a staging copy disallows everything ([robots.txt](docs/guide/seo.md#robotstxt)).

## 0.6.0

1. **Deleting the last User who may manage Users is refused.** `HasKamiRoles` now boots model hooks on your User: `$user->delete()` on the only User holding `kami.users.manage` throws a `ValidationException` and records `kami.users.lockout-refused`. Code or tests that delete that User give the right to another User first, through `AssignRoles` ([The lockout guard](docs/guide/users-and-roles.md#the-lockout-guard)). Creating and deleting a User through Eloquent also writes a row to the Audit log.
2. **Give Roles through `AssignRoles`.** Rows written to `kami_role_user` directly still work, but skip the lockout guard and the Audit log. A seeder giving Roles calls `AssignRoles` instead ([Giving Roles](docs/guide/users-and-roles.md#giving-roles)).
3. **MFA, required for Users who may manage Users.** `KamiPlugin` adds Filament's authenticator-app second factor to the panel. Your User model implements Filament's `HasAppAuthentication` and `HasAppAuthenticationRecovery` with the `HasKamiMfa` trait, or the sign-in page fails ([The user model](docs/guide/users-and-roles.md#the-user-model)); `kami:install` creates `kami_user_mfa`. At their next request, Users holding `kami.users.manage` are sent to set MFA up before any other page, and a test signing such a User in sets MFA up first. The panel gains Filament's profile page when it had none ([MFA](docs/guide/users-and-roles.md#mfa)). `kami:doctor` now exits with 1 when a `->multiFactorAuthentication()` or `->requiresMultiFactorAuthentication()` set on the panel after the plugin replaces Kami's MFA, or when the User model lacks the two contracts, which also stops `kami:install` before it changes anything; it warns, so `--strict` fails, while a User who may manage Users has not set MFA up ([MFA](docs/guide/security.md#mfa)).
4. **A panel signing in through another provider stops `kami:install`.** `kami:doctor` now exits with 1, and `kami:install` stops before changing anything, when the guard of a panel carrying `KamiPlugin` uses another model than `auth.providers.users.model`, which Roles, authorship and the first administrator point at: point the guard's provider to that model ([The user model](docs/guide/users-and-roles.md#the-user-model)). In production, it warns, so `--strict` fails, on an `http://` `APP_URL` or Site URL and on a session cookie not always `Secure`: set `SESSION_SECURE_COOKIE=true` in `.env`, or default `secure` to `true` in production in `config/session.php`, as the starter kit does ([HTTPS in production](docs/guide/security.md#https-in-production)).
5. **Sign-in is throttled per email.** `KamiPlugin` replaces Filament's sign-in page with its own: on top of Filament's five attempts per minute per IP address, five per minute per email, every attempt counting, the MFA code step included. A test or script signing one User in more than twice a minute, with MFA, is refused for the rest of the minute: clear the cache between runs, or sign in with `actingAs()`. A sign-in page class of your own, `->login(MyLoginPage::class)`, keeps Kami's from serving, and `kami:doctor` warns ([Sign-in](docs/guide/security.md#sign-in)). Sign-ins, failed sign-ins of existing accounts and lockouts now write rows to the Audit log.
6. **Pickers take `view` on the target type.** A relation Field's picker and the Parent input now search and title only Entries of types the User may view; a target already picked, of another type, shows as a restricted Entry with no title. Creating a target from a picker now takes the target type's `view` as well as `create`. A Role that should see a type's titles in a picker, or create its Terms from one, grants `kami.entries.{type}.view` ([Permissions](docs/guide/users-and-roles.md#permissions)).

## 0.5.0

1. **`media` is reserved for Media Conversions.** Kami answers `/media/{id}/{version}/{file}` itself. A Content Type whose Prefix starts with `media`, a type with no `$prefix` whose handle is `media` included, now fails the first time the types are read: set another `$prefix`; its Entries move on the next `kami:install`, and their former addresses answer 404. A root slug `media` is refused at its next save; the stored Path keeps answering until then ([Prefix](docs/guide/content-types.md#prefix)).
2. **`kami:install` links `public/media`, and `kami:doctor` checks it.** Apache now serves Conversions without PHP ([Conversions](docs/guide/hosting.md#conversions)). `kami:install` fails when `kami.media.public_disk` is not a local disk, or when `public/media` already exists as a directory or a file: move it out of `public/` first. `kami:doctor` now exits with 1 on a missing link, a non-local public disk, or an `array` or `null` default cache store. A CI job running it runs `kami:install` first, on the `file` or `database` cache store. On nginx, add the configuration from [nginx](docs/guide/hosting.md#nginx).
3. **`/public/media` in `.gitignore`.** The link `kami:install` creates points to the developer's own path: add `/public/media` next to `/public/storage` in the site's `.gitignore`, as the starter kit does.
4. **Originals the web server can serve are refused.** `kami:install` fails before migrating, and `kami:doctor` reports an error, when `kami.media.originals_disk` is a local disk whose `media/` lies under `public/`, under the public disk's `media/` or under a `storage:link` target: the files went out with their EXIF and GPS. Set it to a private disk, such as `local`, and move its `media/` files there ([Conversions](docs/guide/hosting.md#conversions)).

## 0.4.0

1. **`SaveEntry::handle()` takes a sixth argument, `updatedAt`.** It is optional, so no call changes. Passed, a save over a change made since is refused ([Concurrent edits](docs/guide/publishing.md#concurrent-edits)). Every save now also touches the Entry's `updated_at`, a save changing one Translation only too: code reading it sees more recent dates.
2. **`<x-kami::preview-banner />` in your layout.** The admin's new Preview renders the unsaved form through your type's `render()`. Add the component at the top of your layout's `<body>`, so an editor reading a Preview is told so; it renders nothing on public pages ([The Preview banner](docs/guide/routing-and-rendering.md#the-preview-banner)).
3. **`SaveEntry` refuses a `'*'` call with no Entry on a translatable type.** It created an Entry with no Translation, which no visitor read found and which blocked a Singleton's later creation; it now throws an `InvalidArgumentException`. Create the Entry with a Locale first, then write its Shared Fields ([Scope](docs/guide/publishing.md#scope)).
4. **`force-delete` is a new Permission.** A Role granting it through a wildcard, such as `kami.*` or `kami.entries.*.*`, may now delete Entries from the Trash for good. Narrow the Role in `config/kami.php` if it should not ([Permissions](docs/guide/users-and-roles.md#permissions)).

## 0.3.0

0.3.0 is the full content model. The public surface only gains, but some behaviour tightens, so a site that relied on it can stop deploying or see a save refused. Run `kami:install` as usual: its migrations run first.

1. **Drift stops a deploy.** `kami:install` now fails when the declarations and the `kami_type_*` tables disagree, and `kami:doctor` reports the same. A column added by hand, or a Field never migrated, needs a migration or a declaration ([Content model drift](docs/guide/content-types.md#content-model-drift)).
2. **`Entry::query()->cursor()` throws.** It used to return `null` for every stored Field value with no error. Use `lazy()` or `chunk()`.
3. **`SaveEntry` refuses more.** An Entry that is not of the given type; a slug that cannot answer at its URL, such as `a?b#c d`, or that a Locale's code would hide; a value too long for its column; a required Shared Field left blank while a Translation of the Entry is Published. Each but the first is a validation error on the value; the first throws `InvalidArgumentException`. Stored values stay as they are until the next save, which may then ask for a correction.
4. **Scheduled Translations take `publish`.** Editing one, as editing a Published one, now takes the `publish` Permission: a User without it edits Drafts only ([Permissions](docs/guide/users-and-roles.md#permissions)).
5. **MySQL and MariaDB compare Paths by bytes.** `/café` no longer serves the Entry at `/cafe`, and the two slugs no longer clash, as on PostgreSQL and SQLite. A link relying on the old match answers 404.

## 0.2.0

0.2.0 is the walking skeleton: Content Types, Entries in several Locales, Kami's route, Roles and the security headers. 0.1.0 shipped no migration and no content API, so the breaking change in the release notes (`SaveEntry::handle()` and the edited content migration) touches no 0.1 site. What a 0.1 site adds, before its first `kami:install` on 0.2:

1. **The User model.** It implements `KamiUser` with `HasKamiRoles`; `kami:install` stops until it does. Its `canAccessPanel()` admits Kami Role holders only, unless your panel serves other Users too ([Users and roles](docs/guide/users-and-roles.md#the-user-model)).
2. **The configuration.** `php artisan vendor:publish --tag=kami-config`, then declare your Content Types and Locales in `config/kami.php` ([Content types](docs/guide/content-types.md)).
3. **Kami's route.** `Kami::routes()` at the end of `routes/web.php` ([Routing and rendering](docs/guide/routing-and-rendering.md#kamis-route)).
4. **The security headers.** `SecurityHeaders` appended to the `web` group in `bootstrap/app.php`, and `'kami.security-headers:admin'` at the end of the panel's middleware ([Security](docs/guide/security.md#your-own-pages)).
5. **`storage/framework/kami/.gitignore`.** `kami:doctor` writes `doctor.json` there and Kami does not ignore it for you: add a `.gitignore` holding `*` and `!.gitignore`, as the starter kit does.
6. **The first administrator.** `kami:install --no-interaction` now creates one and fails without `--email`, `--name` and the `KAMI_ADMIN_PASSWORD` environment variable ([The first User](docs/guide/installation.md#the-first-user)). A test or deploy script that runs it on an empty database passes them; the starter kit's `tests/Feature/InstallTest.php` shows how. A site with Users passes an existing User's email, with `--email` alone: that User gets the `administrator` Role and keeps their password.

Then run `kami:install` as usual: it migrates, syncs Paths and makes sure a first administrator exists.
